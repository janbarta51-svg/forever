COOKING & WELL FED

Food Buffs

Many cooked foods in Forever also grant +5% experience from enemy kills. Required character level and Cooking skill are different.

Only one Well Fed food buff can be active at a time. Some uncommon recipes have a known Cooking skill but no independently verified minimum character level; these are clearly marked.

SHARE WITH YOUR PARTY

WoW Chat Export

One short line at a time, grouped by class. Each spell shows the level that unlocks its current rank.

About clickable spell links: In-game links copies a short /run command. Paste it into the WoW chat, then press Enter: the game creates real spell links and posts them to your chosen channel. Copy commands individually, outside an active encounter, and make sure you can speak in that channel. Spell IDs may resolve to a base rank or fall back to plain text. Plain text works for everyone. Addon links become clickable for recipients with the optional ForeverBuffLinks companion addon installed; otherwise they see readable text with spell IDs. The addon uses spell IDs for tooltip links, which may show a base rank in the client.

PROFESSION UTILITY

Campfire Buffs

Once you have unlocked camping through The Great Outdoors, you can benefit from other players' profession objects. Buff strength usually scales with character level.

Placing a campfire object requires the relevant profession skill. Nearby players can receive its benefit after spending about a minute by the fire. Some profession buffs conflict with class buffs.
